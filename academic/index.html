About

I am an undergraduate in the Turing Class at Peking University's School of Electronics Engineering and Computer Science. I work on peer prediction — eliciting truthful information from agents when no ground truth is available — with Prof. Yuqing Kong, and on algorithmic game theory with Prof. Xiaotie Deng. More broadly, I am interested in how strategic behavior shapes algorithms, markets, and information systems.

Research Interests

  • Peer Prediction. Designing truthful, incentive-compatible mechanisms for information elicitation without verification — including multi-task settings and the communication complexity of peer prediction.
  • Algorithmic Game Theory. Mechanism design, auction theory, and equilibrium analysis in computational systems where strategic agents interact with algorithms.
